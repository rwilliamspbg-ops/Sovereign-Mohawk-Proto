Disproven as originally stated

The claim that hierarchical composition of per-tier Multi-Krum guards gives “55.5% Byzantine resilience” has a machine-checked counterexample (hierarchical_composition_counterexample): a two-tier tree in which every tier’s honest-majority guard holds while the global Byzantine leaf fraction is 60%. Abstract

We present Sovereign Mohawk, a federated learning (FL) architecture whose formal proofs establish simultaneous guarantees of Byzantine fault tolerance, differential privacy, optimal communication complexity, and cryptographic verifiability for configurations parametrized up to a design target of 10 million nodes. Our core contribution is a proof-driven design methodology that treats formal verification as a constructive tool rather than a post-hoc validation step, yielding a traceability matrix of 19 claim rows that together state exactly which guarantees are machine-checked, which are surrogate or model-level, and which remain open.

The architecture introduces Hierarchical Multi-Krum aggregation. Its earlier “55.5% Byzantine resilience” framing was disproven: a machine-checked counterexample shows a two-tier tree in which every tier’s guard holds while 60% of leaves are Byzantine. What is machine-checked is the single-tier guard arithmetic, a corrected deterministic composition predicate, and a probabilistic Chernoff repair. The hierarchical design is formalized as an $O(d \log n)$ per-update path-depth proxy (total bytes remain model-dependent), and zk-SNARK-based verifiable aggregation is formalized as a constant-cost verifier model — the production verifier checks a fixed genesis identity, not a circuit bound to submitted data.

Scope note: the 10M-node figure is a mathematical parameter in the theorem statements below, formally checked in Lean — it is not a claim that the system has been run at that scale. The largest real, reproducible deployment evidence in this repository is 500 live Kubernetes pods running unmodified orchestrator/node-agent binaries with real mTLS identity and real gradient submissions.

Formal Claim Status

The authoritative per-claim status lives in the traceability matrix: 19 claim rows, each labelled fully_formalized, surrogate_verified, model_verified, measure_theoretic_verified or empirically_verified. Every row is machine-checked about a stated model and its hypotheses — none is a machine-checked statement about the deployed Go binary, and the Go↔Lean correspondence is empirical.

CLAIM 1 Byzantine Resilience

Disproven as stated; the corrected guard is machine-checked

The claim that hierarchical composition of per-tier Multi-Krum guards gives “55.5% Byzantine resilience” has a machine-checked counterexample (hierarchical_composition_counterexample): a two-tier tree in which every tier’s honest-majority guard holds while the global Byzantine leaf fraction is 60%. The 5/9 figure survives only as a single-tier, guard-conditional fact (9f < 5n).

Status: compositional claim disproven deterministically; single-tier arithmetic fully_formalized; corrected predicate fractionSafe (ProtocolSafety.lean) and the deployment-scale Chernoff repair (chernoff_hierarchical_bound_deployment_scale) machine-checked.
CLAIM 2 Differential Privacy

RDP composition closed; Gaussian bound still an axiom

Independent and adaptive RDP composition over the additive ledger is machine-checked, and the (ε, δ)-DP conversion Go’s CheckBudget applies is formalized separately through a computable log-bound sandwich (Refinement/RDPLogBound.lean). The Gaussian mechanism bound is carried as an explicit axiom rather than a derived result.

Status: surrogate_verified_with_gaussian_axiom; core composition closed for independent and adaptive mechanisms; full (ε, δ) conversion no longer a gap.
CLAIM 3 Communication

Logarithmic path depth proved; total bytes model-dependent

The hierarchical per-update path depth is formalized as $O(d \log n)$. A separate compression theorem would be required for any sublinear total-byte claim; the naive uncompressed round is exactly dimension·(n+1)·4 bytes (Refinement/Transport.lean), i.e. $O(dn)$.

Status: fully_formalized for path depth; total-byte compression not proved — the earlier “40 TB → 28 MB” figure on this page had no artifact behind it and has been removed.
CLAIM 4 Straggler / Dropout Resilience

Model-verified liveness guards

Liveness guards are proved about an explicit Chernoff probability model for redundancy levels r = 4, 8, 12 at 90% per-replica availability (Theorem4Liveness.lean, Theorem4ChernoffBounds.lean). Probability-measure and full stochastic formalization remain planned work. A superseded 2025 sign-off that claimed “99.902% > 99.99%” contained an arithmetic error and is marked SUPERSEDED in the repository.

Status: model_verified — proved about the stated probability model, not the deployed runtime.
CLAIM 5 Cryptographic Verifiability

Constant-cost verifier model; not a Groth16/q-SDH formalization

The checked claim is a constant-operation verifier cost model with concrete runtime guards. The production verifier checks a fixed genesis identity, not a circuit bound to submitted gradient/model data, and a full Groth16/q-SDH formalization is out of scope. The Schnorr/Ed25519 identification reduction (SchnorrReduction.lean) is fully formalized algebra modulo an explicit forking step.

Status: model_verified — the 10.55 ms verify figure is a measured runtime number, not a formal constant.
CLAIM 6 Convergence

Envelope guard plus an a.s. result with an explicit open hypothesis

The integer/rational convergence envelope is machine-checked as a numeric guard. The measure-theoretic capstone (MultiKrumConvergence.lean) proves almost-sure convergence under a Robbins–Monro diminishing step size, and states openly that it needs an unbiasedness hypothesis the Multi-Krum selection rule does not supply. The stronger non-convex $O(1/\sqrt{KT})$ bounds remain roadmap work.

Status: measure_theoretic_verified; unbiasedness hypothesis open.
