## How to add an entry

1. Complete a review (issue comment, PR, or email to maintainer).
2. Maintainer adds a row above with name/handle, scope, date, and a link.
3. If the review led to a code change, reference the PR in the Notes column.

## Credits policy

- Every reviewer is credited here and in `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Substantive reviews (new theorem, discharged axiom, found vulnerability) earn
  co-authorship on the resulting technical note or paper.
- Letters of recommendation / LinkedIn endorsements available on request.
